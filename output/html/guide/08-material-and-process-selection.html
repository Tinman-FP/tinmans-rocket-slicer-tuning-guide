Select a matching material and process in Rocket

Rocket's material selector and process selector are linked. Copying a plastic material gives it a new identity. A process that accepts the original material does not automatically accept the copy. If Profile becomes empty and Preview is disabled after you select a copied material, check compatibility before changing printing parameters.

Copy the material

In Rocket 1.4.0.705, open Prepare → Template Settings → More Options → Plastics. Choose the applicable OEM material and use its copy icon. Select the new entry under Customized, give it a clear candidate name, and edit the intended value. For ordinary-plastic flow testing, Basic information → Flow multiplier is the material-wide multiplier. Apply saves the edits. The stock material remains unchanged.

For example, a development-only comparison may use a clearly marked name such as CANDIDATE | GUIDE OFFLINE DO NOT PRINT | 0.4mm | OEM PETG | r01. That is not a production profile or a starting prescription for PCTG. Real tuning names should identify your physical machine, nozzle, product and revision.

Give the copied material a compatible process

Open Profiles in the same options panel. Use the copy icon on the applicable OEM profile-group header. This copies the group's processes; it does not make every copied process appropriate to every mode or nozzle.

Expand the custom group and select the ordinary Speedy process for an FFF comparison. In this SK3 build, custom groups are broadly selectable with copied materials. There is no reachable Compatible materials page in this workflow. Do not mistake the custom group's availability for an enforced match to your machine, nozzle or material; you must check that match yourself.

Use the custom group's rename/pencil action to give it a recognizable candidate name. Return to Prepare and explicitly select the copied Plastic material, the intended print mode and the matching Profile. Confirm that layer height and other settings have not reverted or disappeared. Recheck selections after import, reset or mode change.

Check the slice before sending

Select Preview once and wait for slicing to finish. Review the ordinary-plastic paths, top support and layer count. Print Details should agree with the intended nozzle/material usage, but its labels alone do not prove the executable tool path. Save a local G-code copy and verify the applicable emitted settings and routing.

For the flow pack, tile labels do not change the material multiplier. Use one tile and one explicitly calculated value per slice. Do not confuse the Composite matrix/fiber fields with the ordinary Plastic material. Native projects and G-code can carry account metadata; do not upload raw exports as public test files.
